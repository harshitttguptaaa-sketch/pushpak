Most people redeem the way the bank app makes easiest, because working out the alternatives takes too long. The information exists. It just isn't built for someone with two minutes at checkout.

Too long to read

The real answers sit in 40-minute videos and 300-comment threads. You need one line, not a research project.

Out of date

Banks change earn rates, caps and transfer ratios often. Last year's advice can be wrong today, and most guides never say when they were checked.

Not about your cards

Blogs rank the "best cards" in general. None of them tell you what the points already sitting on your card are worth.

From points balance to a decision in under five minutes.

Pushpak works from your card name and your points balance. That is all it needs.

  1. Pick your card

    Choose it from the supported list. The beta starts with a small set of popular cards.

  2. Enter your balance

    Type in the points you see in your bank app. Nothing is pulled from your account.

  3. Compare every route

    Statement credit, vouchers, airline and hotel transfers, side by side in rupees after fees, with the best value on those figures highlighted.

  4. Redeem with your bank

    Choose what suits you and follow the checklist and link to your bank's own redemption page.

Every figure shows where it came from.

  • Sourced and datedEach value carries the bank's published source and the date it was last verified, so you can check it yourself.
  • Honest about estimatesAirline and hotel transfer values depend on the booking, availability and charges, so they are labelled as estimates.
  • No card numbers, CVVs or bank loginsPushpak never asks for them. Your card name and balance are enough.
  • Your data is not for saleWe don't sell user data, and we don't rank options by what pays us.

Why we're building Pushpak

Your credit card app shows your points balance. But what can you redeem it for in rupees, after fees?

The same points can go to statement credit, vouchers, or airline and hotel transfers, and each route comes with its own fees, minimums and limits. The answer is spread across your bank's site.

Pushpak puts those routes side by side, with the bank's source and the date we last checked it. Then you redeem through your bank, not through us.

We named it after the Pushpaka Vimana, the flying chariot from the Ramayana. Your points are meant to take you places.
